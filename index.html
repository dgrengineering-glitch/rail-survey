Quick start

  1. Install: in Safari tap Share ⬆︎ › Add to Home Screen. Open Rail Survey from the Home Screen icon. Once opened, it works with no signal.
  2. At each location tap Mark Location. GPS starts at once and keeps the most accurate fix for about 10 seconds. Watch the ±metres figure: under 5 m is good.
  3. Tap Accept when happy, or Refine to keep improving it. No signal or permission? Tap Manual and type the lat/long or paste a Google Maps link.
  4. The tag is suggested from your last one (ATS 123 → ATS 124). Tap Dictate for notes, or use the 🎤 key on the keyboard.
  5. Fill in the running-edge distance, ELR, track ID, miles and chains (yards are worked out for you) and any other measurements. Add photos if you like.
  6. Tap Save location. It’s stored on the phone straight away, with or without signal.
  7. Use Summary to search, view, edit or delete, and Export for KMZ/KML (Google Earth), Excel or CSV.

GPS tips

  • Hold the phone up with a clear view of the sky. Cuttings, tunnels, bridges and station canopies make it worse.
  • Precise Location must be on: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Safari Websites › While Using the App + Precise Location.
  • Phone GPS is typically ±3–10 m. Always check critical positions against Network Rail records.
  • If a photo carries GPS data and there’s no fix, the app will offer to use the photo’s location.

Estimate from mileposts

Optional and online only. It asks OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) for mileposts and railway lines near your fix and suggests an ELR, track name and an interpolated mileage. It is approximate – always verify against NR records. Nothing is filled in unless you tap to use it. OpenStreetMap has few UK mileposts mapped, so often you’ll only get an ELR suggestion. Your approximate position is sent to the Overpass service when you tap the button.

Exports & backup

  • KMZ – for Google Earth, with photos inside. KML – smaller, no photos (opens in Google Earth and Google My Maps).
  • Excel – a Summary sheet (one row per location) plus a Measurements sheet. CSV – the same Summary table.
  • Full backup – everything including photos, as one JSON file. Restore merges a backup back in (newer copies win).
  • On iPhone the Share sheet opens – choose Mail, Save to Files, Google Drive etc. On a computer the file downloads.

Google Sheet sync – setup (about 10 minutes, once)

  1. On a computer, go to sheets.new while signed in to your Google account and name the sheet, e.g. “Rail Survey”.
  2. In the sheet choose Extensions › Apps Script. Delete the sample code and paste in the whole of Code.gs from github.com/dgrengineering-glitch/rail-survey › apps-script/Code.gs. Click 💾 Save.
  3. Choose your own secret token (a long random phrase, 16+ characters). In the Apps Script editor pick the function setup in the toolbar and click Run. Approve the permissions (Sheets and Drive, your account only). Reload the Sheet tab and a Rail Survey menu appears: use Rail Survey › Set sync token… and enter your token. (Alternatively type it into the SYNC_TOKEN line near the top of Code.gs.)
  4. In Apps Script click Deploy › New deployment, type ⚙︎ Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec).
  5. In Rail Survey › Settings › Google Sheet sync: tick Sync to my Google Sheet, paste the URL and your token, tap Test connection. You should see “Connected to …”.
  6. Tap Sync now. Records then sync automatically whenever you have signal (on opening the app, when signal returns, after saving). Unsynced records show an orange badge.

“Anyone” means anyone with the long secret URL can reach the script, but it refuses every request without your token. Photos go into a Drive folder called “Rail Survey Photos” and are shared “anyone with the link can view” so the link in the sheet works. If you change Code.gs later, use Deploy › Manage deployments › Edit › Version: New version so the URL stays the same.

Privacy & storage

All survey data is stored only on this device (IndexedDB) unless you switch on Google Sheet sync. Nothing is sent anywhere else, apart from your approximate position when you tap Estimate from mileposts. Clearing Safari website data or deleting the Home Screen app removes the data – export or back up regularly.

Troubleshooting

  • Dictate does nothing: iPhone live dictation in web apps needs Siri & Dictation switched on and often a data connection. Use the 🎤 key on the keyboard instead – it works everywhere.
  • No GPS prompt: close the app fully (swipe up) and reopen, then check the Location settings above.
  • New version: the app updates itself when online; tap Reload when the banner appears.
